## In the region

This month Argentina became the first country in the world to grant legal personhood to companies run entirely by AI agents, without yet having a general AI law in force. The legal paradox is concrete: an "automated company" in Argentina today has more clearly defined legal certainty than any person who interacts with it. The debate between President Milei and writer Yuval Noah Harari, published in the Financial Times, positioned the country as a voluntary laboratory for AI deregulation, at a time when the rest of the region is watching to see which model to adopt for its own frameworks. The unanswered question is what happens when an "automated company" causes harm and its liability is limited to the entity's own assets: there is not yet a clear protection mechanism for affected Latin American citizens.
