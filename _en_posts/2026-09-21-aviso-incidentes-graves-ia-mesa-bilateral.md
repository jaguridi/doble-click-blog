## In 60 seconds

- **What happened.** The proposal came out of a meeting in New York with Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng, ahead of the summit on Thursday the 24th.
- **Why it matters.** Our reading is that the channel through which notices of serious incidents will flow is being set at a table for two.
- **What we don't know yet.** Beijing did not comment: the Chinese delegation left without speaking to the press, so there is a proposal and not an agreement.

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on Sunday that Washington proposed to Beijing a notification mechanism for artificial intelligence incidents that rise to the level of national security, [as CNN reported](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/09/20/business/us-china-trade-talks-ai-intl-hnk). The proposal came out of a meeting in New York with Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng, who is preparing the summit between Donald Trump and Xi Jinping at the White House on Thursday the 24th. Bessent said he was seeking "a shared vision of common objectives and threats" and argued that it matters to move from opacity to greater transparency between the world's first and second artificial intelligence powers.

A notification mechanism defines concrete things: who is notified when a system fails with serious consequences, within what time frame and in how much detail. Our reading is that, for Latin America, that channel weighs more than the content of the agreement. The two countries that concentrate frontier capacity are discussing the plumbing between them, in a bilateral meeting in which, according to this edition's sources, no third party took part. A security agency or data authority from the region would have no way in there. It is not yet known whether the mechanism, if agreed, will notify anyone beyond the two governments, or whether there will be a public text describing it. The opposing reading is that a channel between the two countries where the frontier labs are located can be agreed much faster than a multilateral one, and that its existence is the condition for expanding it later.
