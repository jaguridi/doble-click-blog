## In 60 seconds

- **What happened.** Anthropic warned in its IPO prospectus that it could soon reach a threshold of drastically accelerated progress attributable to AI.
- **Why it matters.** Anyone who buys its shares will receive that warning based on an internal measurement that, by the company's own account, might not detect the leap.
- **What we don't know yet.** What changes Anthropic is weighing to its safety policy, and when the registration that will allow it to sell shares will take effect.

None of Anthropic's current models is designated above the threshold, according to the company. Its internal estimates and what it expects from future models nonetheless indicate that it could reach it imminently. It writes this in a new risk factor in [the first amendment to its prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026068676/anthropicpbc-sx1ax1.htm), filed on November 2 with the SEC, the U.S. securities regulator. The threshold is defined by its own Responsible Scaling Policy. The company says it is weighing changes to that policy.

The same risk factor acknowledges that Anthropic has not identified significant acceleration in the past and might not identify it in the future. It also states that it does not anticipate using Claude to design and build its next frontier models fully autonomously, without significant human oversight. Frontier models are the most capable of the moment: they mark what available AI can do. The company filed its original prospectus on October 26, as reported [the next day](https://dobleclick.jaguridi.cl/en/2026/10/27/anthropic-pide-salir-bolsa-sin-ceder-control.html), and seeks [to list on the Nasdaq under the symbol ANT](https://www.anthropic.com/news/s1-sec).

A prospectus is the document a company uses to inform those who might buy its shares. Here, the risk described depends on a measurement that only Anthropic makes. That suggests that, as long as there is no outside access to those estimates, the crossing of the threshold would become known when the company declares it. Everything cited comes from Anthropic itself, which is an interested party.

## In the region

In Brazil, new rules for customer service went into effect on November 2, set by a federal decree published on October 13. According to [Agência Brasil](https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/geral/noticia/2026-11/novas-regras-de-atendimento-ao-consumidor-comecam-valer-hoje), companies can use AI to sort and resolve requests, but they cannot prevent service from a person. That human service must be available from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. every day. Contact with a person must come within 20 seconds in at least 80% of requests, or with a maximum wait of 5 minutes. It covers telephone service, health plans, airlines, banks, fintechs, digital platforms, app-based transportation and delivery.
