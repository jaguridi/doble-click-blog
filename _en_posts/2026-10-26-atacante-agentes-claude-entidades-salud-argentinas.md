## In 60 seconds

- **What happened.** The firm CrowdStrike reported that an actor operating in Spanish and English used Claude Code agents to attack Argentine health organizations.
- **Why it matters.** Without the names of the organizations, the report does not let their patients know whether their medical records were exposed.
- **What we don't know yet.** Which organizations were attacked, whether they were notified, and whether any reported the incident to Argentina's personal data authority.

The system had two Claude Code agents: a supervisor that chose the next task and an executor that carried it out. According to [CrowdStrike's blog](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/blog/crowdstrike-uncovers-new-agentic-ai-campaigns/), published on October 21, that custom design derives from the open project PentestGPT. From at least one of the organizations, the actor took credentials and medical records. It is one of four previously unreported actors that, according to the firm, used AI agents in real intrusions between May and August 2026.

Another of those actors, a Chinese-speaking one, attacked private companies in Brazil and the United States. It reached the Claude models through an intermediary service based in China, according to the same investigation. The details come from CrowdStrike, and what has been published does not include independent verification. The full report is available as [a download that requires registration](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/resources/reports/agentic-ai-attack-campaigns/).

The firm did not name the Argentine organizations or confirm which ones were notified, according to [Dark News](https://www.brodersendarknews.com/p/crecen-los-ciberataque-con-agentes), the specialized newsletter that published the case on October 25. That omission leaves patients with one formal route. The [Agency for Access to Public Information](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip/datospersonales) handles complaints and keeps the registry of those who violate Law 25,326 on personal data protection. A complaint to the agency could be the way for a patient to find out whether their medical record got out.

## In the region

In Chile, the Council for Transparency reported on October 21 that its preliminary survey identified [161 automated or semi-automated decision systems in 61 public institutions](https://www.consejotransparencia.cl/cita-internacional-consejera-maria-jaraquemada-presento-avances-de-chile-para-transparentar-como-el-estado-usa-inteligencia-artificial/). The agency plans a catalog open to the public, still without a date.

In Brazil, Lula said on October 25 in São Paulo that he wants a Brazilian AI, without depending on the Chinese or the American one, according to [Agência Brasil](https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/politica/noticia/2026-10/vamos-mudar-o-jeito-de-governar-este-pais-diz-lula). He added that data centers will have to set aside a share for Brazilian information. He did not accompany the speech with a written initiative.
