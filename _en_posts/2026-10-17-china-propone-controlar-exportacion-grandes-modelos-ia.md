## In 60 seconds

- **What happened.** China proposed adding AI models, agents and compute clusters to its list of restricted exports, in a draft from two ministries.
- **Why it matters.** A foreign company or government that wants to install a large Chinese model on its own servers could depend on the exporter obtaining a license.
- **What we don't know yet.** What changes the final text will have after the public consultation, and from what date the restriction on models would apply.

The threshold is in the [draft's annex](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.wps). The weights, code and architecture of models trained with 10^26 floating-point operations or more are restricted. Only the most expensive training runs reach that measure of compute used. Models with 2 trillion parameters or more, and those ranked among the top 10 in public global rankings, are also covered. Anything published as open source or in academic papers is excluded.

[Announcement No. 48 of 2026](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/dwmygl/art/2026/art_61446ab4beee4f63973d4d0ab3097648.html) came out on October 16 and covers 40 technologies in AI, biomedicine and photovoltaic energy. Twenty-three are AI, among them large models, agents, compute clusters and information processing systems. Starting that date, exporters must first register on a Ministry of Commerce platform. The [spokesperson's response](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/xwfb/xwfyrth/art/2026/art_d7661dbf62794345a221404f82a25959.html) excludes APIs (the connections other programs use to query a model), web applications and software used as an online service.

That exclusion suggests where the weight of the control would fall. Someone who queries a Chinese model over the internet today would not have to notice any changes. What would come to require a license is handing over the model itself. That would be the case for a university or a ministry in another country that prefers to process its data on local servers. Open-source release could thus become the path with the least paperwork for a Chinese lab to distribute a large model outside the country.
