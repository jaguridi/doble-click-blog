Anthropic published its [second risk report](https://www.anthropic.com/aug-2026-risk-report), 186 pages required by its own internal responsible scaling policy. The most interesting thing is not what the document measures, but what it admits it can no longer measure: the company acknowledges that its instruments for estimating how much its models' capabilities are really advancing have lost precision. That is why it raises the risk of catastrophic harm from misalignment (that is, a model pursuing goals different from the ones it was asked to pursue) from "very low" to "low," and clarifies that the change reflects uncertainty, not a new finding.

Along the way, the report reveals two things that were not on the public radar. The first is Model 2, an internal model somewhat more capable than Mythos 5, in heavy use within the company, which did not go through the full battery of pre-deployment tests and which, according to the text, there are no plans to release. The second is an error that lasted eleven months: between May 2025 and April 2026, a configuration flag left disabled the classifier that blocks dangerous biological information across all human feedback traffic. That amounts to 133 million exchanges with about 50,000 outside contractors.

That second fact is the one that lands in the region. The contractors who align these models (the people who rate responses to teach the system what is right and what is not) are largely workers from the Global South hired through intermediaries. Latin America is already inside the process: it provides labor and is exposed when a filter fails, without having a single institution capable of auditing the report, the internal model it describes, or the scale by which the company scores itself. The document came out on the same day that [the best quarter in its history](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/anthropic-revenue-surges-over-11-210857853.html) became known: $11.5 billion in the second quarter, fourteen times the same period of 2025, its first positive operating result, and an IPO planned for the northern-hemisphere fall. Self-assessment remains the only accountability mechanism available, and it is written by the interested party. For those who want to read it in full, there is the [redacted 186-page PDF](https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/f61d49fa5596956a5dec75fea0e973bf6a6a8378/Redacted%20Risk%20Report%20August%202026%20.pdf), which covers February 24 to July 15, 2026.
