## In 60 seconds

- **What happened.** Anatel will require Brazil's mobile carriers to validate their individual users and individual microentrepreneurs with facial recognition.
- **Why it matters.** The monthly report the standard requires, on how each user was validated, could show how many Brazilians do not appear in biometric databases.
- **What we don't know yet.** Which private biometric database providers the carriers will hire, and how many users will end up validated with an identity document alone.

Face capture will have to include a liveness test at PAD level 2 or higher, under the international standard ISO/IEC 30107-3. That level aims to prevent someone from presenting a photo, a mask, or a video instead of a real face. The image is then compared against a biometric database from the government or a private provider, and the system must detect deepfakes and synthetic content generated by AI. That is what [Technical Standard GT-SEG.2026.11](https://sei.anatel.gov.br/sei/modulos/pesquisa/md_pesq_documento_consulta_externa.php?md_pesq_parametros=HWH32bONvibUcMC3mewfUpIX7e-9fyZZC4iEjI2QHwXAoLCOrVZwNzRf5vR3YcCMWNZ4eCgQDLmVzIOFPcg7RnfQmjuCpYJVYp6GT0FejrRN4sRn2k7ngVcXzkk-vFO2) of the National Telecommunications Agency (Anatel) sets out; the regulator released it on October 28, according to [Teletime](https://teletime.com.br/28/10/2026/anatel-torna-reconhecimento-facial-obrigatorio-na-validacao-de-linhas-moveis/).

New prepaid activations have until September 30, 2027. Postpaid lines, number portability (switching carriers without losing the number), and SIM swaps have until December 31, 2027. Existing lines that have not been validated must be registered again by December 31, 2028. Starting January 1, 2029, they are suspended for 120 days, and starting May 1 they are canceled. Legal entities are exempt.

Starting in September 2027, carriers will report to Anatel every month the validation route used for each user. The standard distinguishes four routes: against a biometric database, with a document only, by exceptional release, or by proxy. The last three do not involve a comparison against a database, so that count would suggest how many people do not appear in those databases. For the data, the standard refers to the General Data Protection Law (LGPD) and to the rules of its enforcement authority, the ANPD. It also prohibits sharing biometric data with third parties outside that law and requires registration data to be kept for five years after the contract ends.
