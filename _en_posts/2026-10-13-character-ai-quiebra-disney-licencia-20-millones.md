## In 60 seconds

- **What happened.** Character Technologies, owner of the chatbot platform Character.ai, started a bankruptcy process to sell all or substantially all of its assets.
- **Why it matters.** Disney is offering to license intellectual property, not to run the service, so whether it continues depends on another buyer coming forward.
- **What we don't know yet.** Which Character.ai rights would be part of Disney's license, and whether any improved bid will include keeping the platform open.

The auction's baseline bid comes from Disney: $20 million plus transition services. In exchange, it is asking for a perpetual, non-exclusive license to certain intellectual property and certain rights of the company, according to [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/bankruptcy-law/character-ai-files-for-chapter-11-bankruptcy-in-delaware). A baseline bid sets the floor of a court-supervised auction, and other buyers can top it. The process is voluntary and falls under Chapter 11, the part of U.S. bankruptcy law that allows assets to be sold under a judge's supervision. The case was filed on October 12 in the Delaware bankruptcy court.

The company attributes the decision to legal, regulatory and operational difficulties, without going into detail, according to [its announcement](https://blog.character.ai/an-update-on-character-ai/). It expects to close the sale in late November or early December 2026. If no bid preserves the service, it will shut it down. For now the service is still running and paid subscriptions remain active. The same announcement reminds users that each of them can download or delete their information.

The shape of Disney's offer suggests who bears the uncertainty. A non-exclusive license allows the use of certain assets without buying the app or taking on its operation. For anyone who pays for a subscription or keeps conversations on the platform, that could mean its continuity depends on a different buyer, one that does not yet appear in what has been published. While the auction lasts, the only thing each user directly controls is whether to take their history with them or delete it.

## In the region
