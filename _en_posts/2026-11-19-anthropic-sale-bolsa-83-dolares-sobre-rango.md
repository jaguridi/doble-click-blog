## In 60 seconds

- **What happened.** Anthropic set the price of the 980 million shares in its initial public offering at $83, with trading expected to begin on November 19.
- **Why it matters.** It sold more shares, at a higher price, than it announced on November 10, and almost all of them are new: that money goes to the company.
- **What we don't know yet.** Whether the underwriting banks will use their option to buy up to 147 million additional shares within the planned 30 days.

Of the Class A shares offered, 956,351,354 are being sold by Anthropic and 23,648,646 by existing shareholders. The company receives no proceeds from the latter, according to its [release](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering) of November 18. The company expects the stock to trade on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol "ANT" and the offering to close on November 20.

The price came in above the $70 to $78 range that Anthropic announced on November 10. That day it launched its investor roadshow with 825 million shares, as we reported [on November 11](https://dobleclick.jaguridi.cl/en/2026/11/11/brasil-pone-docente-entre-ia-alumnos.html). According to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/), the offering raised $81.3 billion, above SpaceX's $75 billion. The same outlet puts the valuation at around $1.5 trillion fully diluted, that is, counting all the shares that could eventually be issued.

Raising both the size and the price at the same time suggests that purchase orders exceeded what the company expected when it opened the roadshow. Because almost the entire offering is new shares, most of the money raised goes into Anthropic's coffers and not to departing partners. Whoever buys ANT gets a share of the business, but not control: the cofounders retain a majority of the vote, according to the prospectus described [on October 27](https://dobleclick.jaguridi.cl/en/2026/10/27/anthropic-pide-salir-bolsa-sin-ceder-control.html). That document also stated that the offering is not registered with the securities regulators of Brazil and Mexico.

## In the region

In Cuba, Resolution 24/2026 of the Ministry of Communications approves a methodology for introducing AI into public administration, published on November 17 in the [Official Gazette](https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2026-o96.pdf). It sets pilots in 2028 and scaling up in 2029 and 2030, with data hosted on national infrastructure or infrastructure governed by sovereign agreements. Each entity will have to identify positions that may become obsolete.

In El Salvador, the [catalog of the U.S. AI exports program](https://aiexports.gov/consortia-menu) includes the American SI Consortium for El Salvador, with Dell as an anchor member. The page does not show the designation date, and [elsalvador.com](https://www.elsalvador.com/noticias/nacional/consorcio-inteligencia-artificial-infraestructura/1304550/2026/) reported it without amounts or timelines.
