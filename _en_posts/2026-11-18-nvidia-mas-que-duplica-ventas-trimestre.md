## In 60 seconds

- **What happened.** Nvidia reported revenue of $115.8 billion for the quarter ended October 25, 103% more than a year earlier.
- **Why it matters.** Its $130 billion outlook excludes China, so the promised growth could end up in the hands of a few cloud operators.
- **What we don't know yet.** Whether the board's new export committee will change how Nvidia sells to customers with ties to China.

In the third quarter of its fiscal year 2027, Nvidia's Data Center segment contributed $107.6 billion. That is a 110% year-over-year increase. Compared with the previous quarter, total revenue rose 20%, and GAAP net income reached $65.3 billion. The figures come from the company's [earnings release](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-third-quarter-fiscal-2027). It was the first report with Colette Kress in charge on an interim basis, following the departure of Jensen Huang reported [on November 13](https://dobleclick.jaguridi.cl/en/2026/11/13/huang-deja-nvidia-directora-financiera-mando-interino.html).

For the fourth quarter, which includes an extra week, Nvidia projects $130 billion, plus or minus 3%. That target assumes no data center compute revenue from China. The expected increase would therefore depend on customers such as CoreWeave, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Oracle and Nebius, which, according to the release, already operate Vera Rubin racks, its newest servers. If demand is concentrated among those few buyers, each quarter could be read in light of how much they spend.

The same day, the board created a standing national security and export control committee, chaired by director Suzanne Nora Johnson, according to [another release](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-of-directors-establishes-national-security-and-export-control-oversight-committee). The trade compliance teams will report directly to it. That committee could be accountable for the licenses imposed by the U.S. rule on AI chips for five countries, Brazil among them, described [on November 17](https://dobleclick.jaguridi.cl/en/2026/11/17/brasil-lista-riesgo-washington-chips-ia.html).

## In the region

In Chile, Science Minister Ximena Lincolao and Sam Altman signed the [OpenAI for Chile](https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-for-chile) memorandum in San Francisco on November 16. The ministry says it involves no cost or exclusivity and that it will not publish the text, according to [Radio Universidad de Chile](https://radio.uchile.cl/2026/11/17/openai-llega-al-estado-chileno-luego-de-un-acuerdo-con-el-ministerio-de-ciencia/).
