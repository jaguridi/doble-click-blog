## In 60 seconds

- **What happened.** Google announced that, starting October 9, anyone who uses the Gemini app without paying for a subscription will have a single model available, Flash-Lite.
- **Why it matters.** Anyone auditing chatbot answers, like the Boca de IA election study in Brazil, will have to record which plan they used for each query.
- **What we don't know yet.** Whether the cut also reaches AI Plus subscribers in Brazil, and on what date, which Google will tell each of them by email.

AI Plus will keep Flash-Lite and Flash, but will lose Pro, according to [the Gemini help page](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17004136). Its subscribers will get an email with the date the change reaches them. AI Pro and AI Ultra keep all three models and will get the Deep Think option. [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/10/03/gemini-model-limits-oct-26/), which reported the change on October 3, gives prices of $4.99 a month for AI Plus and $19.99 for AI Pro.

Each model will have low, medium and high effort levels, and the higher ones use up more of the usage limit. Since May 17, that limit has been calculated by compute, that is, by the processing each answer requires. AI Plus has twice the standard limit and AI Pro four times, according to the same help page. For users, that suggests a trade-off: asking for more effort could leave them fewer queries.

In Brazil, Google launched AI Plus on October 7, 2025, at 24.99 reais a month, according to [TechTudo](https://www.techtudo.com.br/noticias/2025/10/google-ai-plus-estreia-no-brasil-por-menos-que-um-almoco-e-com-gemini-edsoftwares.ghtml). The change affects those who audit chatbots. The Ministério Público Federal (MPF), the federal prosecutor's office, and the ITS Rio center use [the Boca de IA study](https://itsrio.org/pt/publicacoes/boca-de-ia/) to measure how chatbots answer questions about candidates. Its fifth round was covered in [the September 29 entry](https://dobleclick.jaguridi.cl/en/2026/09/29/respuestas-chatbots-brasil-recomendaron-candidatos.html). If there is a runoff, scheduled for October 25, and the measurements continue, a free account and a paid one will no longer query the same model. Comparing one round with another would then require noting the plan used.
