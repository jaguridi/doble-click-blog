Apple closed the largest AI deal in mass-market consumer hardware: Gemini, Google's model, will rebuild Siri from scratch in iOS 27. The announcement came at WWDC 2026, Tim Cook's last keynote as Apple's CEO, with striking numbers: a custom model with 1.2 trillion parameters at a cost of [approximately $1 billion a year](https://letsdatascience.com/news/apple-unveils-gemini-powered-siri-and-ios-27-at-wwdc-2026-b757953c).

The decision does not close off the ecosystem. At the same time, Apple presented an Extensions system that, for the first time in iOS history, lets any user choose between Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini for Apple Intelligence features. The three big frontier AI labs on the same device: a shift that moves the field of competition. It is no longer just about being "the model," but about being the extension the user turns on by default. For Latin America, where the iPhone holds between 30% and 40% of the premium segment in the five largest markets, the concrete effect is that Gemini will arrive as a preinstalled option on millions of devices in fall 2026, while Claude and ChatGPT will be available but will require explicit activation. None of the three models incorporated a regional perspective into its design: the region was not consulted.
