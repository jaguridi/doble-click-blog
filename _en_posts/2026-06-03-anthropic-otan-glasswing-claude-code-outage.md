On June 2, Anthropic [announced the expansion of Project Glasswing](https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-project-glasswing) to 150 new organizations in more than 15 countries, with NATO and ENISA as the program's first multilateral government partners. Glasswing protects critical infrastructure (water, energy, health, communications) from the kind of attacks where AI can be both the weapon and the shield. That same day, Claude Code, the same company's programming tool, suffered [a global outage of almost six hours](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/06/02/claude-celebrates-anthropics-stock-market-float-with-blockbuster-outage/5250071) whose root cause was exactly the kind of failure that most worries experts in agentic AI: subagents that multiplied in an infinite loop until they exhausted the systems.

The paradox has additional layers. The incident occurred the same day Anthropic filed its S-1 with the SEC, the document that precedes its IPO. And there is an absence worth naming: after more than six weeks of operation, no Latin American organization appears on the Glasswing list.

## In the region

Colombia enters the home stretch of its presidential runoff (June 21) with a concrete problem: AI is already active in the campaign, but legal protection will not arrive in time. [Law 2502](https://www.diariobitcoin.com/noticias/la-ia-invade-la-campana-presidencial-de-colombia-con-videos-virales-y-fantasias-electorales/) (which penalizes the use of AI to impersonate identities) is in force, but the specific penalties only take effect in July, ten days after the election. Experts point out that cloned audio is the most urgent threat, above deepfake video, because WhatsApp is the dominant distribution channel and its reach is practically impossible to audit in real time. The contrast with Brazil is sharp: that country approved PL 2338 on May 27 and has five active TSE measures in force for these elections. Against that same backdrop, Meta announces from London that LatAm is one of its main growth markets in conversational AI for businesses, while no country in the region has specific AI regulation for business messaging.
