## In 60 seconds

- **What happened.** OpenAI banned the accounts of Dark Clark, a Russian influence operation focused on Latin American politics and culture, according to its October 8 report.
- **Why it matters.** Fact-checkers were able to debunk individual pieces, but seeing who was behind them required account data that only the company that owns the model has.
- **What we don't know yet.** Whether governments, media outlets or fact-checkers in the region will be able to access that data or receive warnings before a campaign like this gains ground.

The operation had a front with a name of its own: the Social Research Center, a supposed Latin American think tank headed by Mia Clark, a fictitious person. That is how it is described in [OpenAI's report](https://openai.com/index/disrupting-ai-enabled-false-front-operations/), summarized by [CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/openai-disrupts-russia-iran-ai-influence-operations/). According to the company, its employees in the region did not know they were working for a Russian group. The network sought to damage Ukraine's image and meddled in the politics of Argentina and Bolivia. OpenAI rated it a 5 on its own impact scale, which runs from 1 to 6.

Several of its pieces were already known in the region. In Ecuador, the network spread a TikTok video about the recruitment of Ecuadorians for Ukraine, which Lupa Media verified as false in March. It also spread a fake audio clip of the Ukrainian consul. In Bolivia, during the May protests, it spread a fake audio clip about water cuts in La Paz, which the government denied. According to [The Record](https://therecord.media/openai-disrupts-russian-iranian-operations-chatgpt), the operators turned to ChatGPT to create supposed document leaks and scripts for those audio clips.

The attribution to Russia is made by OpenAI with its own data, and the press reports it based on its report. The sequence suggests a practical limit for fact-checkers in Ecuador and Bolivia. They debunked the content in March and May, but the link between those pieces depended on account records beyond their reach. A think tank with real local staff could also get past filters that look at the text and not at who controls the organization.
