The Pentagon runs **La Tilde**, a Spanish-language news site aimed at Latin America that produces content with generative AI at scale, with no bylines, no visible masthead and with its only disclaimer ("funded by the US government") buried on the About page. [The Intercept revealed on June 2](https://theintercept.com/2026/06/02/la-tilde-propaganda-latin-america-pentagon/) that the operator is SOCSOUTH (US Special Operations Command South), the same unit that in April 2026 ran similar operations in Arabic for the Middle East.

What makes the case unique is not just the propaganda itself (that is nothing new) but its economics: the marginal cost of producing geotargeted content with language models approaches zero. La Tilde's editorial mix is deliberate: personal finance guides sit alongside articles praising US military operations in the region. The most powerful actor in the world has already figured out how to direct persuasive content to millions of Spanish-language readers for a practically nonexistent price. The question left on the table: what tools and what budget do fact-checking outlets and LatAm governments have to detect it in real time?
