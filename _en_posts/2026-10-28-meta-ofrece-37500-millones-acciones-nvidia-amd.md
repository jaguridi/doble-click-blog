## In 60 seconds

- **What happened.** On October 26, Meta launched two stock offerings totaling $37.5 billion, and Nvidia and AMD will put up part of the money.
- **Why it matters.** The largest tranche, about 60 billion, will be sold only once a new class of shares begins trading, and that conditions the pace of the financing.
- **What we don't know yet.** When the Class C stock will begin trading and on what terms the sales program tied to it will be agreed.

The [October 27 term sheet](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000119312526432549/d382225dfwp.htm) sets 40 million common shares at $625 each, for 25 billion. They are sold through forward contracts that Meta will settle no later than November 2027. The same document adds 12.5 billion in convertible preferred stock, with a 6.50% dividend. [Nvidia](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-and-meta-to-expand-collaboration-on-muse-and-muse-family-of-models-amid-robust-growth) will invest 3 billion in the offering and [AMD](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1302/amd-and-meta-advance-strategic-partnership-to-power-metas-superintelligence-infrastructure), 1 billion, according to their announcements.

The [initial announcement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000119312526431036/d116268dfwp.htm) filed with the SEC, which regulates the stock market in the United States, links the offerings to Meta's investment in AI infrastructure. Even so, the stated use of the proceeds is general corporate purposes. In that text, Meta projects capital expenditures (what it spends on long-term assets) of $135 billion to $145 billion for 2026. These figures come from documents by the company itself and its suppliers, without an independent assessment.

For those who already own Meta shares, the largest amount is in another tranche. The announcement anticipates an at-the-market sales program of about 60 billion, which would begin once its Class C stock starts trading. That sale has no agreement or terms yet. This suggests that the timeline for the new class could determine when Meta again uses stock to finance spending like this. It could also determine how much its ownership is spread across more shares.

## In the region

In Venezuela, Minister Yván Gil presented the [National AI Plan 2025-2031](https://mincyt.gob.ve/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Plan-Nacional-de-Inteligencia-Artificial.pdf) on October 27. The 81-page document envisions a high-performance computing center running free software and funding for 300 projects, with no budget figures. Its goal of creating an institute with regulatory and oversight functions depends on a law. According to the plan itself, that law was approved in first reading on November 19, 2024.

Colombia's Communications Regulation Commission and SUTEL, its Costa Rican counterpart, signed a [cooperation memorandum](https://www.crcom.gov.co/es/noticias/comunicado-prensa/memorando-entendimiento-sutel-crc) that includes the regulatory implications of AI. It does not transfer powers or create costs.
