Colombia's government and Google presented an artificial intelligence training partnership on October thirteenth. Colombia's Ministry of Information and Communications Technologies reported it in an official statement.

The agreement has three parts. The first is fifteen thousand scholarships for Google Career Certificates, which are taken on the Coursera platform with up to one year of access. They are awarded by the organization Colnodo, with priority for Chocó, Valle del Cauca, Risaralda, Caldas and Quindío, the departments affected by the August earthquake.

The second part is the largest: up to seven million licenses for Google's school tools for public schools, over four years. The first phase will be in Chocó and Buenaventura. The third is training through the public technical apprenticeship service, with a potential reach of one million people.
