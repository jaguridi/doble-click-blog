Anthropic, the company that develops the Claude models, set the price of its initial public offering at eighty-three dollars per share. The company itself announced it in a release on November eighteenth.

The offering is for nine hundred eighty million shares. The company is selling almost all of them, and a smaller portion, about twenty-three and a half million, is being sold by shareholders who already held a stake. Anthropic receives no money for those shares. The company expects the stock to begin trading on November nineteenth on the Nasdaq, under the symbol ANT, and the offering to close on the twentieth.

That figure is the outlet's calculation, not the company's.
