Colombia's Ministry of Information and Communications Technologies demanded a refund on October thirtieth. It amounts to two hundred thirty-four billion, three hundred ninety-nine million pesos. It was the money for the PotencIA Centers, a project of the previous government to teach artificial intelligence.

The project promised seventy-five learning centers in twenty-six departments, for more than seven million people. Later, the figure discussed was sixty centers. The contract with Findeter was signed in September twenty twenty-four, and all the funds were transferred to a trust company, an entity that manages funds on behalf of others.

According to the ministry's statement, no center is operating. The same text cites the Office of the Comptroller General of the Republic. It measured construction progress of two point zero nine percent and a complete standstill since November twenty twenty-five. Minister Alexandra Falla Zerrate said she was acting on the instructions of President Abelardo De La Espriella.
