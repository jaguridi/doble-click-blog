A committee of the United Nations General Assembly approved on November twenty-fourth a text supporting two UN bodies dedicated to artificial intelligence. They are the Independent International Scientific Panel and the Global Dialogue on Governance.

The vote was split: seventy-nine votes in favor, fifty-seven against and forty-seven abstentions, according to the official UN record. The text was introduced by Russia, and it affirms the organization's universal convening role in the discussion of artificial intelligence in the non-military domain.

The European Union voted against. Its delegation said the text departs from a previous compromise. The United States said it sought to preserve the mandate of the co-chairs of the Dialogue.

In the region, the votes were divided. Mexico voted in favor and called for respecting the mandates of the panel and the Dialogue. Paraguay voted against. Colombia, Uruguay and El Salvador abstained.
