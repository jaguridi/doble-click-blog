OpenAI reported on October eighth that it banned the accounts of Dark Clark, a Russian influence operation focused on the politics and culture of Latin America. That is what the company's own report says, and cybersecurity outlets such as CyberScoop and The Record reported on it afterward.

According to OpenAI, the operation used a front: the Social Research Center, a supposed Latin American think tank headed by Mia Clark, a fictitious person. The company says its employees in the region did not know they were working for a Russian group. The network sought to damage Ukraine's image and meddled in the politics of Argentina and Bolivia.

Some of its pieces were already known. In Ecuador, it spread a TikTok video about the recruitment of Ecuadorians for Ukraine, which the fact-checker Lupa Media rated as false in March. It also spread a fake audio clip of the Ukrainian consul. In Bolivia, during the May protests, it spread a fake audio clip about water cuts in La Paz, which the government denied. According to The Record, the operators turned to ChatGPT to create supposed document leaks and scripts for those audio clips. OpenAI gave the operation a five on its own impact scale, which runs from one to six.
