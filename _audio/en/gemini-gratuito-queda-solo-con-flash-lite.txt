Starting October ninth, anyone who uses the Gemini app without paying for a subscription will have a single model available, the one called Flash Lite. That is what Google's help page says.

The change also affects the paid plans. AI Plus will keep Flash Lite and Flash, but will lose the Pro model. Its subscribers will get an email with the date the change reaches them. The AI Pro and AI Ultra plans keep all three models and add an option called Deep Think. According to the specialized site 9to5Google, AI Plus costs four dollars and ninety-nine cents a month, and AI Pro, nineteen dollars and ninety-nine cents.

Each model will have three effort levels: low, medium and high. The higher levels use up more of the usage limit, which Google calculates based on the processing each answer requires. As an interpretation, that suggests users might have to choose between asking for more effort and keeping more queries.
